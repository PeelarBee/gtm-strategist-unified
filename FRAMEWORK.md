# General Go to Market Framework

Use this workflow for any company or industry, adapting the work to the product, business model, customer, stage, evidence and constraints. It connects twelve phases rather than assuming a single type of launch.

## Establish context

Understand what is sold, to whom, which problem it solves, existing alternatives, current traction, resources and the decision needed. Separate evidence from hypotheses. Reuse the project's earlier outputs and interview only for task-critical missing inputs.

## Select the relevant phase

| Phase | Decision area | Typical output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Foundations | Direction and constraints | Strategic canvas and 90-day plan |
| 2 Intelligence | Market, customer and competition | Interview and competitor findings |
| 3 Validation | Which assumptions hold | Experiments and evidence-based customer profile |
| 4 Product | What to build and measure | MVP, roadmap and measurement plan |
| 5 Pricing | Value, package and commercial model | Pricing hypothesis and research |
| 6 Positioning | Why the right buyer should care | Positioning and messaging |
| 7 Launch assets | What buyers and stakeholders need | Website, demo and pitch materials |
| 8 Communication | How to reach and convert buyers | Channels, funnel, budget and launch plan |
| 9 Launch execution | How to coordinate release activity | Owned execution plan and results |
| 10 GTM system | What becomes repeatable | CRM, growth experiments and operating procedures |
| 11 Marketing | How to sustain demand and engagement | Content and channel programs |
| 12 Sales | How to qualify and progress buying decisions | Sales assets, outreach and account plans |

Read the selected original task through [SKILL.md](SKILL.md). Use the [reference library](LIBRARY.md) only where it improves the task. Work sequentially when dependencies matter, or jump to a needed decision while labeling missing evidence.

## Produce one decision and artifact

State the goal, relevant facts, alternatives, recommendation, assumptions and next validation action. Tie claims to evidence and avoid completing template fields with invented numbers. Tailor the deliverable to the actual customer and operating model.

## Review and execute within scope

Identify accountable people, dependencies, timelines, metrics and unresolved risks. Track required human approval separately from draft completion. External actions require actual authorization. Where release, activation and market communication differ, plan and approve them separately.

## Measure and update

Define each selected measure with baseline, target, population or denominator, data source, owner and period. Mark unavailable inputs explicitly. Review observed results against assumptions, decide whether to continue, adjust or stop, and save the learning for later phases. Do not confuse activity with customer value or attribution with causation.

## Reuse across industries

Keep this core free of company cases and domain-specific defaults. A separately supplied industry extension can add terminology, workflows, specialist review and evidence requirements while reusing the general phases and library. No industry extension is included in this repository.
